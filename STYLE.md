# Approved visual language

Only these two treatments are in scope; the handler selects the appropriate one for the current short. Neither has a permanent primary/secondary ranking.

**T04 embroidered textile:** visible woven fabric, applique cloth figures, stitched edges, economical expressive faces, deliberate material depth. It must look constructed from textile, not paper with a fabric overlay.

**T02 layered cut paper:** layered cut shapes, tactile paper edges, physically coherent shadows and intentional crafted depth. Preserve the supplied reference's material language without copying its setting into every story.

Use real approved masters supplied separately under `references/` or as image attachments. These descriptions do not substitute for exact reference assets. If exact references are unavailable, identify the limitation; do not claim a newly generated approximation is approved.

Compose before generating: define the story job, emotional contrast, scale, depth, diagonals, negative space and meaningful motifs. Epic imagery requires intentional relationships, not merely a tiny person on a blank field. Stable poses and restrained environment/camera motion are preferable to unconvincing character animation. Keep room for legible Hindi captions.

Symbolic judgment or teaching imagery may be ambitious, but label it visibly as symbolism. Do not introduce unsupported demons, torture, geography, worship gestures or new plot events as source facts. Show only the people needed in that frame and explicitly distinguish whom a gesture addresses or targets.
